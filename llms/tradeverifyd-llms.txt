# Tradeverifyd

> Tradeverifyd is a supply chain intelligence platform (formed by the combination of Transmute and mesur.io, backed by Techstars) that screens suppliers against 200+ global data sources, maps multi-tier supplier relationship graphs, monitors risk events, and issues W3C Verifiable Credentials that can be presented to US Customs (CBP). Coverage spans UFLPA, EU CSDDD, UK Modern Slavery Act, OFAC, EU/UN sanctions, NDAA, and EUDR. The REST API (v1) authenticates with an `ocp-apim-subscription-key` header.

Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-07-21 (method: generated — no provider-published llms.txt was found at tradeverifyd.com/llms.txt).
